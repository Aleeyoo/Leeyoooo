# All Roads Lead to Decentralized AI

![](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HJU-PvnaMAAVBnK.jpg)

We’re living in a world where AI is accessible every where. Roughly 18% of the global population is using AI. This number increased from ~16% late 2025 and ~15% early 2025.

The pace of AI adoption is rapidly accelerating. More apparently so on the enterprise front — 9 out of 10 Fortune companies overspend their budget on Claude Code by 500% vs their annual budgets while traditional SaaS net dollar value dropped 15%, signaling that enterprises are cutting software to spend on AI.

**Menlo Ventures** reported $1.7B to $37B Enterprise AI growth in their 2025 end of year report. The number probably looks very different now — 1H 2026 is likely $35-40B+ driven by rapid adoption of AI agents along with the models serving them.

![](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HJU9XMMacAAwyvE.jpg)

In 2024, around **53% of enterprises chose to buy AI** solutions instead of building their own. In 2025, that number jumped to **76%**. In 2026, I believe we’ll see **85%-90%** given how easy & seamless it is to integrate Claude or Codex into existing enterprise workflows.

The **AI vendor lock-in is real** and the frontier labs are getting real good at this. They create many great tools for both technical & non-technical professionals, set up headquarters on key cities globally, send forward-deployed engineers to top corporations, and lock in multi-year enterprise contracts as a result.

On the consumer side, they subsidize the credits, allowing anyone to get more value out of their subscriptions. No wonder why people continue subscribing to ChatGPT/Codex or Claude despite the high price ($20-$200 per month).

For those who can’t afford to pay $200/month or those who prefer their data/prompts to stay private, they opt for open-source models which can be installed and use locally/privately (for free as long as u got a good GPU) OR use the models via gateway like OpenRouter or Venice.

> The AI Commodity TAM continues to rapidly grow BUT the value are mostly accruing in private markets (to frontier labs, to open labs). While public markets like **decentralized AI and onchain AI** continue to be highly **accessible** + provide **asymmetrical** opportunities to back the future of AI.
In this piece, we’ll go over the structural tailwinds for decentralized AI and why I believe we’ll see a huge shift in the landscape this year.

## **1. Mythos found 10,000 bugs across 1,000+ systems but only 97 patched**

Mythos is considered a highly advanced frontier model that’s so good at security & autonomous hacking ability that the model is restricted to only a handful of Anthropic corporate partners.

Project Glasswing where Anthropic gave 50+ partners early access to Mythos to find vulnerabilities. In the first month alone, 10,000 vulnerabilities was found. Cloudflare alone has 2,000+ (400+ high/critical) but only 97 of the total 10,000 was patched so far.

A single bug in a core library affect thousands of downstream apps/integrations. Engineers need to test every patch to make sure it doesn’t crash existing systems.

Lots of vulnerabilities happen on open-source libraries which are usually maintained by open-source devs, hobbyists, most are volunteers or underpaid.

Even when it’s not open-source bugs, enterprise engineers & application security engineers are in shortage. They gotta hit deadlines + also fix bugs, most are overworked.

**This is where decentralized AI comes in**

- @bittensor subnets can incentivize engineers across the world to contribute and get paid based on their work
- @bankrbot and @virtuals_io help open-source developers bootstrap funding to continue their work
**Opportunities: **Despite the lack of narrative tailwinds, AI security remains crucial yet underappreciated. DeFi hacks & exploits happen on a daily basis — audit companies, AI security solutions, Bittensor security subnets (e.g. Trishool, BitMind, MIID/Yanez, Aurelius, Bitsec, Redteam) will be more important in combating hacks, exploits, deepfakes, and more.
