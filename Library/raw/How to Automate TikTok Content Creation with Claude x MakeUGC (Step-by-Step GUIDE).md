## WHY SLIDESHOWS BEAT EVERY OTHER FORMAT RIGHT NOW

The swipe mechanic keeps viewers inside your content instead of passively watching it. When someone swipes through 6 slides, they are moving through your product story at their own pace. This shift in control stops the content from feeling like an ad—it feels like a discovery.

Beyond the algorithm, slideshows are dramatically cheaper. By using **MakeUGC** instead of manual editing, you remove the design bottleneck entirely. You can post at a volume that's realistic only for a machine, and volume is what TikTok rewards.

## STEP 1 — FIND WHAT'S ALREADY WORKING

Don't try to invent a format. Search keywords in your niche and filter by **Most Liked** or **Most Recent**. Look specifically for slideshows that pass these 4 tests:

- **\> 100,000 views** — real validation.
- **Posted within 30 days** — format is still fresh.
- **Easy to recreate** — no complex 3D assets needed.
- **Repeatable** — multiple accounts are using the same style.

Download the winner using SnapTik to remove the watermark.

## STEP 2 — EXTRACT THE HOOK WITH CLAUDE OPUS 4.7

Upload the downloaded slideshow images to **Claude Opus 4.7**. Claude acts as your creative director, breaking down the psychology of why the post went viral.

**Use this prompt:**

> Analyze this TikTok slideshow and: Identify the main hook used in the first slide. Explain why this hook works (curiosity / pain point / relatability). Write 7 hook variations for my niche: \[YOUR NICHE\]. Give me 5 specific Pinterest search queries to find background images that match this aesthetic.

Save every output. You are building a "Hook Library" that ensures you never run out of proven angles.

## STEP 3 — SOURCE IMAGES FROM PINTEREST

Pinterest is a free, endless library of high-quality images. Use the exact queries Claude generated.

- **Portrait (9:16) ratio** is preferred.
- **High Contrast:** Bold images stop the scroll; soft pastels often get ignored.
- **Outcome Focused:** If you sell a fitness product, source "aspirational morning routine" shots, not just a photo of the product on a shelf.

## STEP 4 — GENERATE SLIDES WITH MAKEUGC

This is where the manual work dies. Instead of opening Canva and dragging text boxes for hours, you use **MakeUGC** to generate your slides programmatically.

**MakeUGC** allows you to input your Pinterest images and the hooks generated by Claude to create a consistent, high-converting aesthetic.

**The Slide Structure:**

1. **Slide 1: HOOK** → The "Scroll Stopper."
2. **Slide 2: Problem** → Agitate the pain.
3. **Slides 3-5: The Value** → Tips, insights, or product benefits.
4. **Slide 6: CTA** → "Check the link in bio" or "Comment for the routine."

By using **MakeUGC**, you ensure the text-to-image ratio is perfect for TikTok’s UI, preventing your text from being covered by the "Like" and "Comment" buttons.

## STEP 5 — SCHEDULE WITH POSTIZ AGENT CLI

With your **MakeUGC** exports ready, use Postiz to handle the distribution.

\# Example batch-schedule logic # One command. Entire week scheduled. postiz posts:create -c "My morning routine ☀️ [#skincare](https://x.com/search?q=%23skincare&src=hashtag_click)" -m "./makeugc\_output/slide\_01.png" -s "2026-04-30T09:00:00Z"

## STEP 6 — POST FROM DRAFTS (THE GOLDEN RULE)

**Never publish directly via API.** TikTok can flag server-side uploads as "robotic," which kills your reach.

**The Hybrid Workflow:**

1. **Postiz** manages the calendar and sends you a notification.
2. The notification arrives on your phone.
3. Open TikTok. Your content is waiting in **Drafts**.
4. **Tap Post.**

From TikTok’s perspective, a human just published from a real device. This preserves your account health while allowing you to automate 95% of the heavy lifting.

## THE STRATEGY LAYER — MAKING IT CONVERT

All of the above is infrastructure. The creative strategy is what drives revenue.

- **For Amazon/Shopify:** The product is the natural next step, not the hero. The content provides the value; the product provides the solution.
- **Human-Centric Copy:** Tell Claude to write like a real person, not a marketer. Avoid words like "revolutionary" or "game-changing."
- **Track Revenue per Million:** If you get 1M views but $0 in sales, the "integration" is wrong. Use Claude to brainstorm how to weave the product more deeply into the value slides of your **MakeUGC** templates.

**The result:** A distribution machine that scales without scaling your budget. You aren't just making content; you're building a system that wins.
