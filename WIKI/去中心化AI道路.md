### 关键引述

> The AI Commodity TAM continues to rapidly grow BUT the value are mostly accruing in private markets (to frontier labs, to open labs). While public markets like decentralized AI and onchain AI continue to be highly accessible + provide asymmetrical opportunities to back the future of AI.

> A single bug in a core library affect thousands of downstream apps/integrations. Engineers need to test every patch to make sure it doesn't crash existing systems.

> Lots of vulnerabilities happen on open-source libraries which are usually maintained by open-source devs, hobbyists, most are volunteers or underpaid.
